How priority is assigned

Manual SOS always creates a high priority event. Gesture and scream scores of at least 0.80 together also create a high priority event.

A single signal of at least 0.70 creates a moderate priority event. Other observations update camera health only.

People counts do not determine danger. The score is a rule-based index, not a validated probability.
