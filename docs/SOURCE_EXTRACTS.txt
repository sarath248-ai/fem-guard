Proposed Solution

We are developing a surveillance camera system specifically designed to prioritize women's safety by integrating advanced technologies. 
This system will feature the ability to count the number of men and women in its field of view using computer vision and deep learning algorithms. 
Emotion detection will be implemented to assess the emotional state of individuals, identifying signs of distress such as fear or anger using facial recognition models. The system will also measure the distance between men and women in the area to detect any potentially unsafe proximity.
In cases of detected distress, a scream alert mechanism will be activated, and an AI voice will promptly ask the woman if she needs assistance. If the woman doesn’t respond, the system will automatically escalate the situation by connecting her to the nearest police station or security center. 
This escalation will include sending live CCTV footage, location data, and event metadata to ensure a quick response. 
Additionally, there will be a button installed in the streetlight lamp posts, allowing individuals to manually connect to the nearest station if they feel unsafe, similar to the safety systems. 
The system will also securely store recordings as evidence for any potential investigation. 
This project aims to create a comprehensive, real-time surveillance solution to enhance the safety and security of women in public and private spaces.

AI FOR WOMEN'S SAFETY: REAL-TIME THREAT DETECTION AND PREVENTION
SLIDE 2
The Global Importance of Women's Safety:

Women's safety is a critical global issue, with millions facing threats of violence, harassment, and assault in both public and private spaces. Despite advancements in traditional surveillance systems like CCTV and security patrols, these measures remain largely reactive, often failing to prevent crimes or intervene in real time. The increasing scale of the problem, with 1 in 3 women globally experiencing physical violence, underscores the need for more intelligent safety solutions. Traditional methods struggle with limited coverage and slow response times, highlighting the necessity for proactive technologies that can predict, prevent, and respond to threats before they escalate.
The Alarming Scale of the Problem:

Globally, 1 in 3 women experience physical violence in their lifetime.
In 2023, reports showed that 35% of women worldwide had been subjected to 
harassment or assault in public spaces.
Workplace harassment affects 60% of women in certain regions, contributing 
to unsafe work environments.
These statistics highlight the urgency of addressing women’s safety with more robust measures.

AI Capabilities:

Pattern Recognition: AI can learn from past events to identify suspicious behavior patterns, such as stalking or loitering, and flag potential threats early.
Predictive Analytics: Using historical data, AI can forecast areas and times where incidents are more likely to occur, allowing for more focused interventions.
Computer Vision: AI-powered cameras can detect unusual activities, alert authorities in real time, and even recognize faces or objects involved in crimes, enhancing security precision.

AI offers a powerful solution to women's safety concerns by enabling real-time data processing, detecting anomalies, and providing scalable coverage across large areas. Unlike traditional surveillance systems, which are reactive and rely on human intervention, AI continuously monitors environments and identifies risks early, allowing for faster and more proactive responses. Its ability to analyze vast amounts of data without fatigue ensures more comprehensive and efficient safety measures.
SLIDE 4
The software integrates with existing surveillance systems to automatically detect potential threats and alert nearby security centers in real-time.(By using a Artificial Intelligence and Deep learning).
High-risk incidents are recorded and stored as non-deletable evidence to ensure the footage is preserved for future legal or investigative purposes.
The software continuously monitors and detects the gender of individuals in the surveillance area, providing a real-time count of men and women present.(also detect dangerous objects).
It can identify suspicious gestures or SOS signals, triggering an immediate alert to local police stations or nearby security centers to initiate a response.
 The system is designed to detect potentially dangerous scenarios, such as a lone woman in the area or a woman surrounded by men, enabling precautionary actions to ensure her safety.
Equipped with an audio detection system, the software can recognize screams and cross-reference them with ongoing gestures in the scene, to accurately assess and respond to threatening situations.
 By training the model with a vast dataset of screams, the software can detect emotions like fear or distress based on sound frequencies. This audio-based emotion detection enhances threat assessment by cross-referencing with gestures for more accurate alerts.
The software operates on a graded threat level system:
             1. Low: Minimal concern, no action required.
            2.Moderate: Situations are temporarily stored for review
            3.High: Serious danger, and footage is saved permanently as secure evidence for later use.
